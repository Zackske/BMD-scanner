# BMD Scan 3.5.0

Reference-driven Home redesign: high-resolution transparent chrome/orbit icons, a bundled Nunito variable font for the rounded BMD ScanTovenaar heading and translated tile labels, smooth themed luminous curved lines, and deeper curved glass tile highlights. The original Blue Medical and Translumina footer assets, their transparency, and the shared Home/Settings placement remain unchanged.

Six persistent themes: Blue Medical, Translumina, Dark, Light, Lime and Dark violet. Theme names follow the selected app language. Translumina keeps the same blue/green/purple/orange Home tiles as Blue Medical, and blue/purple/amber Settings tiles. Dark and Light retain neutral/pastel materials. Lime and Dark violet use coordinated tinted backgrounds, materials, icon accents, header illustrations, light curves and controls. Lot identities and camera images are never recoloured by UI-theme logic.

The theme picker uses brand marks, moon, sun, lime leaf and violet gemstone symbols. Existing saved theme IDs remain valid. New artwork is bundled locally; no network is needed at runtime. Nunito is distributed under the included SIL OFL licence.

Native checks exercise all six themes, transparent high-resolution asset loading, language preview, matching logo placement, camera binding and rotation, alongside the existing scanner/export checks. Camera recognition and cross-photo identity logic have not been changed. Real full-carton 95/200-product recognition and physical Samsung testing are still not validated. No automatic shipment approval.
