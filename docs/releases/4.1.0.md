# BMD Scan 4.1.0

The live scanner now uses the approved uncluttered toolbar: back, lamp, code-format dropdown and settings. The title has been removed from that bar. The compact dropdown offers QR, Barcode and QR + Barcode, with matching icons. QR mode continues to include Data Matrix product codes.

The four large outer scanning corners are removed; individual detection borders, numbered checks, lot colours and unresolved-code indicators remain. The right-hand zoom retains its spring and ripple animation.

The central save control is round with a fine blue double rim matching the gallery pictogram, a document icon containing a downward arrow and a localized Save label. Its press animation and the capture feedback are retained. The gallery pictogram uses a single solid accent without white stroke segments. The save rim and gallery icon match: blue in Blue Medical, red in Translumina, green in Green, purple in Purple, and the corresponding accent in Light and Dark. Lot identities and their colours remain unchanged.

The lamp is white when off and gold when on, with a spring motion and short activation pulse. Its displayed state follows the camera hardware, unavailable lamps are disabled, duplicate requests are guarded and leaving the camera turns the torch off. The settings gear turns before opening. Reduced-motion system preferences are respected.

The full-box marker workflow and direct stack workflow remain intact. Expected-count checks, product grouping, distinct identical products, retake replacement and local/share export remain unchanged.

Release validation covers lint, JVM tests, APK package/signature and native Android emulator tests, including selecting each code format, actual camera capture, retake and persistence. Lamp-on/off rendering screenshots are previews of the native control; an emulator without a torch cannot validate a physical lamp. A real Samsung and a 200-product carton still require field testing.
