# BMD Scan 4.7.0 — Private customer portal

- The home customer tile opens the finished offline portal, with national colours, uniformly sized country outlines, flags and shipping groups.
- Customer records are not included in the public source code or APK. Import a schema-1 JSON register once using the Android document picker; records stay in private app storage excluded from Android backups.
- Invalid, oversized or cancelled imports preserve the existing register. Linked customers remain separate even when they share a customer number.
- Customer details retain full names and supplied fields; compact tile labels fit phone screens. Ten languages and all six themes are supported.
- CI checks compilation, lint, unit tests, APK signing and Android emulator tests. Portal tests and public screenshots use synthetic records only. Physical-device camera and private-register acceptance remain field checks.
