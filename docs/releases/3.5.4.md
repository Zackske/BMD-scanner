# BMD Scan 3.5.4

- Scan menu now has a single magical Scan heading in the same theme gradient as the Home wordmark, with matching scanner and smoke. The duplicate heading is removed; Home keeps its own BMD ScanTovenaar header.
- All four scan menu titles are localized in Dutch, English, Ukrainian, Turkish, Italian, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Hindi.
- Dutch tiles read QR-/aantalcontrole, Doos-/palletcontrole, E-mailcontrole and Documentcontrole. Camera and placeholder headings use the same localized names.
- Retains the consistent translucent black language/theme labels, stronger red text and all six themes.

Recognition and product grouping are unchanged in this presentation update.

- Scan menu title is Scan in Dutch and uses a localized equivalent in other languages. The Home Scannen button is unchanged.
- Bottom menu tiles are swapped completely: Documentcontrole is on the left with its document icon and blue tile; E-mailcontrole is on the right with its envelope icon and purple tile. Both actions follow the swapped tiles.

- Scan menu heading uses the same localized wording as the Home scan tile in all ten languages (Scannen in Dutch, Scan in English).
- The full Document control tile is now bottom-left; Email control is bottom-right, including their colours, icons and actions.

- Home Scan tile now uses a new transparent chrome handheld scanner illustration, with the same cyan orbit and gold sparkle style as the other Home icons. The QR/count tile keeps its QR icon; themed header scanners are unchanged.
