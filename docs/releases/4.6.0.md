# BMD Scan 4.6.0

Eindcontrole now opens a real picklist intake before the quantity and product camera stages. Picklists can be captured by the app camera or imported as multi-page PDF/photos. Bundled on-device OCR reconstructs the product table; every source line/page must be reviewed before the reference is confirmed. Duplicate source pages, missing numbered pages and printed-total mismatches are guarded. Recognized lines can be corrected, added or removed with changes retained in the evidence.

The confirmed quantity drives a balanced carton plan: standard 70 x 50 x 30 cm / 113 products or large 80 x 50 x 30 cm / 125 products. 556 products yields five cartons (112, 111, 111, 111, 111), with a green carton-count statistic. The proposed per-carton quantities can be edited before capture; their sum must match the picklist and each quantity must fit the selected carton. Each carton retains an independent five-photo session and source linkage. Final comparison checks article, lot, expiry and quantities across all cartons. A GTIN-to-article link requires explicit operator confirmation; a lot or matching grand total alone never identifies an article. Completed control evidence can be saved or shared as a ZIP with source documents, CSV/JSON comparison and carton evidence. This does not create an invoice or automatically approve shipment.

Wizard labels are Eindcontrole and Losse stapel (localized in all ten languages). All six themes share the new native flow. Marker guidance now shows A/B above and C/D below, inset inside the carton on top of the preserved red product cartons. Product codes must remain visible; the existing registration/manual alignment checks and animations remain.

Validation includes unit tests for table parsing, totals, missing/duplicate pages, expiry and capacity boundaries; Android OCR of an anonymous seven-line PDF; manual confirmation, recreation, GTIN/expiry mismatch checks; all 60 language/theme combinations; and the existing camera/zoom/marker and baseline regression suites. Native screenshots are retained with the CI run. Real Galaxy camera OCR/focus and live recognition of a full 113/125-product carton still require field testing. OCR was exercised on a generated anonymous table matching the supplied document columns; customer documents are not committed to the public repository.
