# BMD Scan 4.2.0

The scanner adopts the approved slim dock: integrated count and gallery controls with a prominent round Save button, brushed-silver rim and document/down-arrow icon. The gear and plus/minus use the same brushed metal treatment. Backgrounds and accents follow all six themes; lot colours retain their meaning.

The toolbar now places the icon-only code selector before the lamp. A three-dot button opens a compact three-icon menu for QR/Data Matrix, barcodes or both. Visible code-mode words and the downward selector arrow have been removed; accessible labels remain translated.

Zoom is horizontal, centred directly above Save, and focuses on zooming out. Available stops come from the camera hardware: 0.5x, 0.75x and 1x appear only where supported. A device whose camera minimum is 1x shows 1x and cannot select fictional ultra-wide stops. Zoom uses the existing spring and directional ripple feedback, with no simulated camera scaling.

The lamp pulse, gear turn, save press and popup transitions remain, respecting system animation preferences. The camera supports portrait and landscape; menus remain portrait. Full-box marker/overview/detail steps and direct stack scanning remain intact, including capture, review, retake replacement and sharing.

Validation covers JVM bounds tests, lint, APK package/signature, and the native Android camera/UI suite: icon-only format selection, real QR/barcode recognition, capture, retake, persistence and all six palettes. The emulator cannot validate a physical torch or a real ultra-wide lens. Field testing on the Samsung and real product cartons remains necessary.
