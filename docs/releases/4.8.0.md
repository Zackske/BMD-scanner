# BMD Scan 4.8.0

The original Blue Medical home design is retained, with the scanner, translated wordmark, smoke and stars centred together. Scan and Settings restore Home's header spacing and edge-to-edge curves while keeping clear space after the Glass arrow.

- Four scan tiles: final control, pallet/box, document control and general QR scanner. Pallet/box and document control display Work in progress.
- Picking and final control both begin with a reviewed picklist. Picking supports loose stacks; final control asks whether to use the marked carton workflow or inspect before packing.
- The general reader opens its camera directly and reads ordinary QR codes and barcodes independently of product checking. Code positions guide autofocus; pinch zoom, the lamp, gallery input and scanner settings remain available.
- The private customer portal retains continuous scrolling, search, Previous/Next controls, five vertical shipping tabs and fitting email addresses. Customer records are imported privately; no customer registry is bundled in this public APK.
- Layouts and wording support the existing ten languages and six themes.

The build runs Android compilation, lint, unit tests and emulator checks before publishing. Physical-device performance and real shipment accuracy still need workplace testing. This is a test build; it does not certify a shipment or replace the operator's final check.
