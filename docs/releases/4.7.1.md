# BMD Scan 4.7.1

- One consistent round frosted back button across menus, live scanning, picklists, the scan wizard and customer pages. Colours follow the selected theme, with press feedback and translated accessibility labels.
- The customer portal title sits at the top. Search filters locally by customer name, full registered name, country or customer number, and survives returning from customer details.
- Updated country badges, spacing and readable customer tiles. Spain, Portugal and South Africa use enlarged mainland outlines.
- Customer records remain private: they are imported on the device and are excluded from the public APK and source code. An existing private register is preserved when updating the app.
- Six themes and ten languages are supported. Automated checks cover compilation, lint, unit tests, package signing and Android emulator flows. Camera performance on real warehouse products still needs a physical-device field check.
